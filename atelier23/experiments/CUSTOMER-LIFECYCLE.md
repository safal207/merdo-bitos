# Customer Lifecycle Simulator 9.1 — experimental
Baseline 9.0 (392d8da). One synthetic customer; 365 integer days (0–364), no actual identities, currency EUR in minor units, no actual orders, inventory verification, notifications, CRM or external effects. Uses the production experimental graph, activation, learning, trust and opportunity-window modules without modifying them. A calendar is tracked daily; the decision/window evaluator runs at seven explicit episode dates, not all day/offer combinations.
Seven episodes: new professional role day 20, event day 100, decline day 180, impossible fulfillment day 220, stale availability day 260, unknown spatial context day 300, fit review day 340. Dates, decay=2/day, activation strength=90, lead times and prices are synthetic test choices, not Atelier 23 data. Daily activation clocks advance independently of communication; each discussion ends and new episodes require explicit permission. Learning assessed on the first episode only; later understanding unknown after communication restart. Separate simulated feedback on days 40, 130, 350; neither payment nor refund implies satisfaction. The graph checks prior paid order for repeat paths.
Finance: explicit paid fixtures at days 20,100,340 (120000,160000,20000 cents), full refund at 350 (20000). Three historical paid events, two repeats of prior paid events, two retained orders; repeat counts are not reduced by refund (separate retained metric). Costs 168000, acquisition 10000, net revenue 280000, contribution 102000 cents. Not net profit or LTV. No currency mixing; safe integer arithmetic, duplicate/conflicting event IDs, duplicate orders/episodes, time reversal, over-refunds and mismatched authorization rejected. Authorization is a synthetic in-process test contract, not security/payment infrastructure. No payments made automatically by evaluator.
Tests: deterministic replay, daily state/finance invariants, calendar boundaries, transfer vs unknown, decline, stale and unknown context, recurrence, full/partial feedback, payment/refund accounting, negatives, overflow, explicit financial guard. Run node scripts/atelier23-customer-lifecycle.mjs. Future: validate assumptions and separate actual paid, delivered and satisfaction evidence; real LTV requires cohorts and retention. No client UI integration in 9.1.
