# Customer Universe 9.2 — synthetic social purchase rhythms
Baseline 868d243. One market fixture (Lisbon, EUR minor units); no real customers or sales. 1000 synthetic clients, 365 days, explicit seed. This is a simulation, not a forecast, psychological profile, or estimate of Atelier 23 capacity. Moscow must be modelled separately in RUB; no currency mixing.
Social habits are hypothesized planning routines, not universal social laws:
- professional change / planning for first day or important meetings;
- preparation for celebrations (including weddings) with time for fittings;
- seasonal wardrobe review;
- trip and change of setting;
- aftercare after retained purchase.
A social trigger is not a purchase. Budget is an explicitly synthetic annual spending envelope, independent of demographics. We do not infer payday, wealth, relationship status, income, ethnicity, or exploit financial distress. No religious or cultural calendar assumptions. Seasonality windows, price, budgets, probabilities, inventory and production capacity are arbitrary test inputs. Actual lead times and assortment need owner confirmation.
Each episode uses existing graph, activation, learning, communication, opportunity and ledger modules. Learning does not authorize buying. A synthetic payment requires a separate fixture, explicit discussion request, matching context, timely availability, valid graph path, sufficient budget and shared production slots. Decline/explore does not produce a payment. Need intensity decays between episode dates; daily production/finance aggregates cover all 365 days, not daily assessment of every possible need. Monthly buckets use equal 365/12 partitions (not real civil months). Orders consume synthetic inventory and one capacity slot per day of lead time; refunds do not restore consumed resources or sunk cost. Aftercare only after prior retained paid order. Historical repeat paid orders and retained orders are separate. No LTV, net profit or causal marketing result. No deployment, CRM, payment, external message.
